### Economy contract

- Soldier maintenance/upkeep is **removed**: recurring soldier cost is exactly `$0/s`.
- Passive income remains `$10/s` pending export-state calibration.
- An E fortress retains its existing `$2/s` income bonus.
- Rank-quality passive-income bonuses are removed so stronger armies do not automatically mint more money merely for owning expensive troops.
- Kill bounty remains 50% of the defeated soldier's purchase price.
- Starting treasury remains `$175`; this is deliberately unchanged until export evidence shows a problem.
- Fortress upgrades and army-stat training retain their existing costs for this first economy slice.
- Only currently unlocked F/E/D/C soldiers are purchasable. Future prices and profiles are authoritative data, but B through SSS+ Type V remain locked to their roadmap phases.

### HP and mana contract

- Every soldier has authoritative `maxHp`, `hp`, `maxMana`, and `mana` state derived from rank.
- Direct purchases spawn at full rank HP and mana.
- Promotion preserves earned damage, applies the normal +20 XP heal, and grants the difference between the old and new rank maximum HP/mana instead of silently full-healing.
- Basic musket fire costs no mana.
- E-or-higher bayonet charge costs 8 mana.
- C formal-volley shots cost 3 mana; insufficient mana falls back to an ordinary musket shot instead of preventing fire.
- Mana regenerates continuously at the rank-specific rate.
- Future rank-specific abilities remain separate roadmap work; the very high future prices are not justified solely by raw HP/range and must later receive distinct abilities.

### Range and shielding contract

- Every successive rank has strictly longer musket range.
- Organized SIEGE/BREACH fortress fire may also use the soldier's rank range.
- Lower ranks occupy progressively more forward formation layers during ordinary ADVANCE/HOLD/DEFEND/VOLLEY behavior.
- SIEGE, BREACH, CHARGE, TURN, RALLY, and REGROUP movement is excluded from forced rank layering to avoid overriding explicit command mechanics.
- Target selection recognizes lower-rank soldiers positioned in front of higher-rank soldiers as screens. A shield within 125 horizontal units and 90 vertical units strongly discourages bypass targeting.
- The system does not make low ranks invulnerable, teleport units, or globally force all fire onto F soldiers.

### Acceptance criteria

- Exact 15-rank price table is exposed in runtime state and export.
- `purchasableRanks` remains F/E/D/C only.
- Upkeep and upkeep pressure are zero for both armies.
- F/E/D/C purchases deduct exactly $15/$30/$45/$60.
- Spawned soldiers receive exact HP/mana/range profiles.
- Mana is consumed and regenerated by controlled tests.
- Lower-rank shield targeting and forward layering reproduce in controlled tests.
- Existing 150-soldier, 14/company, 11-company, fortress, finite-value, rank, command-authority, and fieldwork-logistics invariants remain valid.
- Export filename/payload and package version use `3.5.0`.
- Multi-seed economy, ecology, natural-siege, and long-war results are recorded as calibration evidence, not predeclared balance success.

### Verification method

- JavaScript syntax checks for changed source and tests.
- Focused Playwright tests for prices, no upkeep, HP/mana/range, mana spending, export versioning, shielding, and formation layers.
- Existing full deployed Playwright suite updated only where the authorized contract intentionally changed.
- Four 600-second economy samples.
- Nine 900-second natural-siege samples.
- Three 2,200-second long-war samples retained as a diagnostic gate; v3.5 is not called balanced merely because mechanical tests pass.
