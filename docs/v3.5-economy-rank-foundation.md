# v3.5 Economy and Rank Foundation

## Outcome

This document records the user-authorized major contract change. It is an implementation specification and evidence ledger, not proof of final balance.

## Corrections and assumptions

- The final rank is **SSS+ Type V**, correcting the submitted `SSS Type V` typo to match the established 15-rank roadmap.
- B through SSS+ Type V remain locked. Their prices and profiles are defined now so later rank implementations use one authoritative table.
- Mana is made functional immediately through existing E bayonet and C formal-volley mechanics. Future rank-specific mana abilities are deferred.
- Starting treasury, passive income, fortress bonus, bounty percentage, fortress upgrade price, and stat-training prices are held constant for the first export-calibration round unless explicitly listed otherwise.

## Implemented economy

- F/E/D/C purchase prices: $15/$30/$45/$60.
- Future prices: B $75, A $90, S $150, SS $300, SSS $450, SSS+ $600, Type I $750, Type II $900, Type III $1,500, Type IV $3,000, Type V $15,000.
- Soldier maintenance: $0/s.
- Passive income: $10/s.
- E-fortress bonus: $2/s.
- Kill bounty: 50% of defeated-rank price.
- Rank-quality passive-income bonus: removed.

## Implemented combat profiles

The exact HP, mana, regeneration, range, and formation offsets are maintained in `docs/roadmap.md` and runtime `state.rankSystem.profiles`.

## Export calibration fields

Exports now expose:

- complete price/profile table
- unlocked and locked rank lists
- maintenance-removal flag
- income formula and live rates
- actor `maxHp`, `hp`, `maxMana`, `mana`, and rank range
- shielding/rank-layer policy
- all prior economy, company, fortress, siege, and training telemetry

## Required interpretation

- Mechanical correctness and balance are separate.
- Zero upkeep is intentionally implemented even if initial runs accumulate excessive treasury.
- Excess treasury, rank flooding, weak mana usage, or siege changes should be tuned from multiple exports rather than silently reverting the requested contract.
- A future high-rank price is not yet a promise that raw HP/range alone makes that rank worth the price; unique abilities remain necessary roadmap work.
