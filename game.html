Phase 3 Rules
• Phase 1 F Class and Phase 2 E Class are stable. Phase 3 unlocks D Class while C and above remain locked.
• F costs $10, E costs $32 and D costs $80. Kill bounty remains 50% of defeated rank price: $5 / $16 / $40. The Phase-2 income and 1.60% army-value upkeep baseline are preserved.
• Every unlocked rank keeps the dual path: General AI may buy it directly or a surviving soldier may earn it through XP.
• F promotes to E at 4 total XP. E promotes to D at 10 total XP. Every earned XP restores 20 HP up to full health.
• D inherits the E automatic bayonet charge without extra bayonet damage. Its Phase-3 identity is an assault drill: under SIEGE, BREACH, or commander CHARGE orders D gains +3.5 percentage points musket aim and a 2-second reload drill bonus with a 25-second D reload floor. Outside those assault orders, D uses an E-equivalent veteran combat baseline.
• General AI does not routinely replace F/E with D. D has a 0% routine target and up to about 8% during SIEGE, purchased only as a funded near-full-army top-off with low upkeep pressure and surplus cash.
• Existing local command, physical regrouping, counter-charge, BRACE, staging, fieldwork recruitment blocking and BREACH siege logic are preserved.
• A musket still has a 30-second base reload. D assault drill modifies D troops only while the assault order is active; no global reload reduction was introduced.
• Commander Form I remains Shii-Cho and is stable. Form II Makashi is the next commander form but remains locked during the initial D-Class foundation release.
• Form I still has no projectile deflection or advanced powers; later forms retain the established Form II–VII identities.
• Each side still owns an F fortress upgradeable to E. Phase 3 does not add a D fortress in the same release as D musketeers.
• Paid recruitment remains blocked while a viable enemy BREACH contests a fieldwork; once relieved, recruits physically deploy from the fortress.
• The battlefield remains 3× long and horizontally scrollable. Restart creates a fresh randomized run.
