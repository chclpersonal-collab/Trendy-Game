Phase 2 Rules
• Two autonomous General AIs manage the war.
• Experimental pricing patch 11/15 is a calibration hold: F remains $10, E remains $32, base income remains $10/s, upkeep remains 1.60% of living army value per second, and kill bounty remains 50% of rank price: $5 for F and $16 for E.
• E remains directly buyable as well as earnable through battlefield XP. Future unlocked ranks keep the same dual-path rule; only F and E are unlocked in Phase 2.
• F soldiers promote to E after 4 earned XP. Every earned XP still restores 20 HP. Directly purchased E soldiers begin at E·0 and retain the automatic bayonet charge.
• General AI considers army quality, upkeep and command integrity before expanding, attacking or committing to a siege.
• Command integrity is the share of active companies that still have a living commander. If too much of the command structure collapses, the General temporarily shifts toward DEFEND until free replacements restore control.
• Siege escalation now uses a committed assault window: once a viable siege begins, the General keeps pressure on the enemy fortress long enough for companies to physically reach it unless command integrity, army strength or home-fortress safety collapses. The most forward viable company becomes the BREACH company while the others screen nearby defenders.
• Commanders issue advance, hold, defend, volley, turn, rally, counter-charge, BRACE and siege orders. If a commander dies, that company becomes less accurate and can panic until a free replacement arrives.
• A musket now has a 30-second base reload after firing. Veteran experience may shave only a small capped amount from that cycle, so a fired line remains vulnerable for most of the reload window.
• Commanders can recognize a nearby enemy line with most muskets still reloading and order a short COUNTER-CHARGE. F musketeers gain a temporary low-power bayonet/melee push only during that order; E musketeers retain their much faster and stronger autonomous bayonet charge.
• A reloading company that detects an incoming charge can answer with BRACE. Braced troops stop advancing, hold their line, use weak defensive bayonet thrusts at close range, and take modestly less melee damage while the charge passes. Reloading continues during the brace.
• Commander Form I is strictly Form I — Shii-Cho. It favors deliberate line protection, broad sweeps against multiple nearby opponents and basic disarming actions. It is deliberately less efficient against a lone opponent.
• Shii-Cho currently sweeps up to three clustered enemies. Its first surviving non-commander target can be temporarily disarmed, preventing musket fire and bayonet charging while the soldier regains control.
• Form I still has no projectile deflection or advanced powers; later forms are locked to the established Form II–VII identities rather than invented forms.
• Each side owns an F fortress that may be upgraded to E. Destroying a fortress wins the war and another begins automatically.
• Restart creates a fresh randomized run instead of repeating the same battle seed.
• The battlefield is 3× longer. Swipe/drag horizontally to side-scroll; use Front to recenter on the current frontline.
• Soldiers can turn around to engage enemies behind them, and companies use marching cohesion so groups are less likely to pass one another or outrun their commander.
• Ordinary movement is now physically speed-limited. Rally, defend, retreat, formation correction and commander regrouping cannot snap actors across large distances; E bayonet charge remains the intentionally fast sprint.
• Replacement commanders deploy from a safe rear fieldwork when possible, fall back to the fortress muster if that fieldwork is threatened, and then physically march back into command range before restoring orders.
• Command remains local: musketeers outside a commander’s practical command radius lose precise orders and the accuracy benefit. If their company commander is still functioning and no immediate enemy blocks them, those stragglers now physically march back toward command range instead of remaining detached indefinitely.
• Companies reduced to four or fewer musketeers automatically stage behind the front until rebuilt to eight, unless an immediate rear threat or siege demands action.
• Experimental prices/economy rules may continue changing through patch 15 and afterward if playtests justify it.
